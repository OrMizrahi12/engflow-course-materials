Thanks for raising this. Linking groups to an account is something we've decided not to do: Spliit has no accounts, and a group's link is its access control. The full reasoning is in [`.out-of-scope/accounts.md`](https://github.com/OrMizrahi12/spliit/blob/main/.out-of-scope/accounts.md) (see also [ADR-0001](https://github.com/OrMizrahi12/spliit/blob/main/docs/adr/0001-no-accounts-link-is-access.md)).

Ways to keep revisiting old groups without an account:

- **Same device:** groups you've opened stay in this browser's recent list; you can star or archive them instead of losing them.
- **Other devices:** open (or bookmark) the same group links there, or keep the links in a note.
- **Accounts on top of Spliit:** a fork, or a separate frontend that talks to the Spliit API, can add account handling without changing Spliit's no-account core, as discussed upstream.

Closing as won't fix.
