# Spliit

Spliit lets a group of people record shared expenses and see who owes whom. It has no user accounts: anyone with a group's link can view and edit the group.

## Language

### People

**Participant**:
A named person inside one group. Nothing links participants across groups, so the same human in two groups is two unrelated participants.
_Avoid_: User, member, friend, account

**Active user**:
The participant that this browser has said it is for a given group. It is a per-device choice, not an identity.
_Avoid_: Current user, me, logged-in user

### Expenses and splitting

**Split mode**:
The rule for dividing an expense among the participants it was paid for: Evenly, By shares, By percentage, or By amount.

**Split value**:
The number entered for one participant on an expense. The split mode decides how to read it: a weight (By shares), a percentage (By percentage) or an amount (By amount).
_Avoid_: Share (for the entered number), weight

**Share**:
The whole number of minor units that one participant is charged for one expense. An expense's shares always add up to exactly its amount.
_Avoid_: Portion, cut

**Income**:
An expense with a negative amount: money the group received, split among participants the same way as a cost.
_Avoid_: Refund, credit

**Category**:
A fixed label for an expense, belonging to a category grouping (for example "Uncategorized / General").

**Document**:
An image attached to an expense.
_Avoid_: Attachment, image

**Receipt scan**:
Creating an expense from a photo of a receipt.

### Recurring expenses

**Recurring expense**:
A series of expenses repeated daily, weekly or monthly.
_Avoid_: Subscription, schedule

**Frame**:
One expense generated by a recurring expense. Once created it is an independent expense, so editing or deleting it leaves earlier frames untouched.
_Avoid_: Occurrence, instance

### Currency

**Group currency**:
The currency a group keeps its books in. Every balance is in the group currency.

**Original amount**:
What an expense cost in the currency it was actually paid in, when that differs from the group currency. It is kept for reference only; the group-currency amount is what counts.

**Original currency**:
The currency an expense was actually paid in, when that differs from the group currency.

**Conversion rate**:
How many units of group currency one unit of original currency is worth, for one expense.

### Balances and settling up

**Balance**:
What a participant has paid minus their total share of the group's expenses. A positive balance means the group owes them money, and a negative one means they owe the group.
_Avoid_: Debt, credit

**Reimbursement**:
An expense that records one participant actually paying another back.
_Avoid_: Settlement, payback, transfer, debt

**Suggested reimbursement**:
A payment from one participant to another that Spliit proposes so the group can settle its balances. It is computed from the balances and never stored.
_Avoid_: Settlement, debt, transfer

### Groups on this device

**Recent groups**:
The groups this browser has opened, stored only on this device.

**Starred groups**:
Groups pinned to the top of this browser's group list. Starring is per device and invisible to anyone else.
_Avoid_: Favorite

**Archived groups**:
Groups hidden from this browser's main group list. Archiving is per device; the group is unchanged for everyone else.

### Group history

**Activity**:
The group's log of changes: expenses created, updated or deleted, group details updated, deletion scheduled, group restored.
_Avoid_: History, audit log

### Group lifecycle

**Scheduled deletion**:
The state of a group whose deletion has been requested. The group stays usable and can be restored until its grace period ends.
_Avoid_: Delete (on its own, for groups), soft delete

**Grace period**:
The 30 days between a group's scheduled deletion and its purge.

**Restore**:
To cancel a scheduled deletion before its grace period ends.
_Avoid_: Undelete, recover

**Purge**:
The permanent removal of a group whose grace period has ended, along with all its expenses and uploaded documents.
_Avoid_: Hard delete
