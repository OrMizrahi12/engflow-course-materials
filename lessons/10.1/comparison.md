# Spliit's glossary next to Potluck's

What Claude showed after the comparison prompt in lesson 10.1, in our words. Headline: Potluck uses different words for
five of Spliit's ideas, and one word, **Participant**, means different things in the two glossaries.

| Spliit | Potluck | Same idea or a clash? |
|---|---|---|
| Participant (anyone named in a group) | Member | Same idea, different word |
| Participant | Participant (a member one expense is split among) | **Same word, different meaning**: Potluck's sense is narrower, closer to Spliit's "the participants an expense was paid for" |
| Reimbursement | Settlement | Same idea, and each glossary lists the other's word as one to avoid. In Spliit a reimbursement is a kind of expense |
| Suggested reimbursement | Suggested settlement | Same idea, different word |
| Original amount + Original currency | Foreign amount | Same idea; Potluck lists "original amount" as a word to avoid, and uses its term for settlements too |
| Conversion rate | Exchange rate | Same idea, different word |
| (only in ADR-0001, not in the glossary) | Group link | Same idea; Spliit has no glossary term for it |

Share, Balance and Group currency mean the same in both, down to which sign of a balance means what. Spliit has nothing
like Potluck's Inactive member, and Spliit's Archived groups only looks related: it is kept per device and applies to
groups, not members.

It changed nothing, because the standing rule for this repository was "rename nothing". When you work across the two
projects, keep both glossaries open, and when someone says "participant", ask which one.
