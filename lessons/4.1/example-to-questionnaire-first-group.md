# How our flat splits money: questions for Potluck

**Purpose:** I'm building Potluck, an app for splitting shared costs, and our flat will be the first group to use it. Before I build it, I need to know whether two of its basic rules fit how we really live: every group uses **one currency**, and every expense has **one person who paid**. Your answers decide whether those rules stay or whether I change the plan first.

**From:** your flatmate who's building Potluck, **To:** the flatmate who keeps track of our money, **How your answers will be used:** to decide what the first version of Potluck can and can't do. Nothing you write goes into the app itself.

## Context

Potluck is a simple website. One of us creates a group, everyone in the group can add what they paid for, and the app works out who owes whom and the fewest payments needed to even things up. Nobody pays through the app; it only records what happened. You know better than anyone how our flat actually handles shared costs, at home and on trips, so you're the best person to say where these rules would get in the way.

## How to answer

Please answer by **[date]**. It should take about 15 minutes. Short answers are fine. Real examples ("in Lisbon, Dan paid for…") are the most useful. If you don't know or aren't sure, say so rather than skipping the question.
